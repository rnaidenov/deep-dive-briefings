Each tap opens a branch and shows its note. Tap again to fold it. Dashed lines link ideas across branches. On a keyboard, Tab into the map, then use the arrow keys, Enter and Esc.

Moby-Dick (Herman Melville, 1851)

  • Story arc
    • Ishmael goes to sea (Ch. 1)
    • A bed shared with Queequeg (Ch. 3)
    • Ahab's oath on the quarter-deck (Ch. 36)
    • The three-day chase (Ch. 133–135)
    • Ishmael alone survives, on Queequeg's coffin (Epilogue)
  • Characters: Ishmael (narrator), Captain Ahab, Queequeg, Starbuck (first mate), Moby Dick
  • Themes: obsession, fate vs free will, the limits of knowledge, friendship across cultures
  • Key ideas: the cetology chapters (Ch. 32), one coin and many readings (Ch. 99), the whiteness of the whale (Ch. 42)
  • Places: the Pequod, New Bedford and Nantucket
  • Quotes
    • “Call me Ishmael.” (Ch. 1)
    • “All visible objects, man, are but as pasteboard masks.” (Ch. 36)
    • “To the last I grapple with thee…” (Ch. 135)
How it's built

One line of markup

Use it in a topic Add 'mindmap' to the loader's script list, put your map in <slug>/mindmap.json, and add <div data-mindmap="mindmap.json"> with a .no-js outline inside. The data format is in _kit/README.md.
